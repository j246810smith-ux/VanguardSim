## The user's feedback (do this first)

1. **"The cards are too small to read on my phone."**
2. **"I want the game to take up my whole phone screen."** The stage is 16:9 (1920 × 1080) but the
   phone is about 20:9, so there are side bars (letterbox) even in full-screen mode.

Recommended fix, one piece of work:

- **Fill the width:** on Android (or any screen wider than 16:9) widen the stage instead of
  letterboxing: keep STAGE_H = 1080, make the stage width `1080 × screen aspect` (about 2424 on the
  Pixel), and lay the battle table, hand and side panels out against that width (`board/layout.ts`
  COL values, `App.tsx` Stage / `stageFit.ts`). The extra width also gives room for bigger cards.
- **Bigger cards on phones:** scale card sizes up on small screens (CARD_RC / CARD_VC and the hand
  card size, plus hand spread), e.g. 1.3–1.5× when the stage scale is under 0.5, and reduce the
  table tilt or use the flat view by default on phones.
- **Read a card:** long press / the Inspect button already open the large details panel; check it
  is big enough on the phone (it should fill most of the screen height).
- Take screenshots with `adb shell screencap -p /sdcard/s.png` + `adb pull` (PowerShell `>` corrupts
  binary output).
