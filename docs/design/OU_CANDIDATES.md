# OU candidates: G era cards for Original Era (Expanded)

Written 2026-11-11 by the card-sets session for workstream W1 of the six-workstream brief
(docs/design/SIX_WORKSTREAMS_AUDIT.md). **Report only:** `data/ou/ou-list.json` is not changed; the owner
approves. Pool: every G era set in the game plus the sets on branch `sets/g-td09` (G-TD01 to G-TD08,
G-BT01 to G-BT09: 1086 cards).

## Method

1. **Automatic blockers** (the same scan as `ouBlockers` in `src/engine/rules/ouList.ts`): G units, scripts
   using Generation Break, the G zone, G units, hearts, Stride, Hollow or G guardians, and card text naming
   them. **615 cards are blocked; 471 have no blocker.**
2. **Human judgement on the 471:**
   - **G era keywords** (Charge, Resist, Oracle, Thunderstrike, Magia, Brave, Bloom, Rush, Unite, Blaze, Burst,
     Darkness, Revelation, Ritual, Wave, Rescue, Time Leap, G era Legion): the engine runs them in any format,
     but they are defined only in the G era rules (CR 1.48.1). Marked **unsure** as a group: the owner
     decides whether OU should include G era keywords at all.
   - **Names only G units have** ("if you have a vanguard with X", where only G units are named X): legal but
     useless in Original Era. **Reject.**
   - **Names only G era normal units have**: fine if those cards are OU too. **Approve** (noted).
   - **Strong effects** were read one by one (guard restrictions, mass retires, extra criticals, locks, delete,
     binds). Almost all are effects Original Era already has (Link Joker locks, Deletor delete, Limit Break
     rides). Exceptions are listed under "Individual decisions".
   - **Power curve:** G era base power is not above Original Era (grade 1 boosts are 6000–7000 against
     Original Era 8000s, grade 3s are 10000–11000 as in BT17), so plain G era units are no power creep.

## The brief's three examples

### G-BT04-020 Maiden of Rambling Rose

**Unsure.** No automatic blocker, but its Legion is the G era "[Legion 22000]" (fixed power, CR 1.48.1); Original Era Legion (CR 1.36) adds the two units' power. It plays correctly in this game either way. Approve only if OU accepts G era Legion wording.

### G-BT03-001 Phantom Blaster Dragon

**Approve.** No G dependency: Limit Break 4 (Original Era), a ride trigger and a search. A G era reprint-style redesign of Phantom Blaster Dragon at Original Era power.

### G-BT10-022 Vollmond Blaukluger

**Not in the pool yet** (G-BT10 is not imported). Review it when G-BT10 is added; the scan above runs on it
automatically (`npm run ou -- check G-BT10-022`).

## Rule differences to keep in mind

- **G era sentinels** print "You may only have up to four cards with [CONT]:Sentinel in a deck". Original Era
  (CR 1.29) has no such limit, so OU sentinels carry their own limit; Original Era perfect guards stay
  unlimited. Approve them, but the deck validator must count only cards with that text (it already reads the
  card's text for "up to sixteen").
- **G era Legion** ("[Legion 20000]"/"[Legion 22000]"): see Rambling Rose above.
- **G era triggers** (critical/draw/stand/heal, shield 10000, power 4000–5000) behave the same. The G era
  "vanguard attacks" critical triggers name G unit vanguards (Gurguit, Gavrail…): rejected below as dead.
- **"Updated after the G era" cards** use their G era text here (D-034); OU should use the same text.

## Individual decisions

- **G-BT07-008 Wings of Recurrence, Blade Wing Reijy**: Blade Wing Reijy: +15000/[Critical] +2 for [Soul-Blast 5] and a rides-from-soul loop; strong for Original Era power levels.
- **G-BT08-036 Barking Dragon Tamer**: Barking Dragon Tamer: "you cannot normal call" is not enforced by this game yet (UNRESOLVED_RULINGS).
- **G-BT04-020 Maiden of Rambling Rose**: Maiden of Rambling Rose: a G era "[Legion 22000]" (CR 1.48.1 Legion sets a fixed legion power); Original Era Legion (CR 1.36) works differently. The engine handles it either way, but the rule text belongs to the G era.
